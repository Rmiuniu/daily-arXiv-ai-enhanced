Method: 以正规双曲算子为基准建立判据：两个 Hadamard 两点函数之差必为光滑齐次双解，故由重排微扰源产生的非光滑项只能归因于算子改变或不可容许的齐次谱数据。对两个脉冲平面波背景，直接从度规重算波算子、模式、世界函数与 Van Vleck 行列式，并用精确模式匹配与几何构造相互比对。

Result: 对第一个剖面，精确模式匹配与几何构造在二阶内一致；对第二个剖面，修正度规使领头微扰由二次变为关于波参数的一次，并消除横向动量倒数极点，同时修正后的度规保留精确对齐的零辐射爱因斯坦源。两例中奇异格林函数恰为 Hadamard 拟基本解的微扰展开，额外对数来自动量奇异的齐次数据；对可精确求解的第一剖面，标准平坦归一化平面波方案给出脉冲面之外消失的重整化应力张量。两剖面的光滑前沿正则化在计算阶数内、且远离特征零模处，重现匹配脉冲传播子的奇异结构而不产生横向动量倒数极点。

Conclusion: 谱和中的表观短距离奇异差异多为表示自由度；只有当算子改变或采用不可容许齐次谱数据时才对应真实态变化，额外的对数项可由此完全解释。

Abstract: Perturbative mode equations often admit formally different particular solutions whose spectral sums appear to have different short-distance singularities. We formulate a general diagnostic which separates this representational freedom from a genuine change of quantum state: for a fixed normally hyperbolic operator, two Hadamard two-point functions can differ only by a smooth homogeneous bisolution. Thus, a nonsmooth term generated by rearranging a perturbative source must be traced either to a changed operator or to inadmissible homogeneous spectral data. We apply the criterion to two impulsive plane-wave backgrounds. Starting directly from the metrics, we recompute the wave operators, modes, world functions, and Van Vleck determinants. Exact-mode matching and the geometrical construction agree through second order for the first profile. In the second, correcting the metric changes the leading perturbation from quadratic to linear order in the wave parameter and eliminates inverse transverse-momentum poles, while the corrected metric retains an exact aligned null-radiation Einstein source. In both examples the singular Green function is precisely the perturbative expansion of the Hadamard parametrix; the additional logarithms arise from momentum-singular homogeneous data. For the exactly soluble first profile, the standard flat-normalized plane-wave prescription gives a vanishing renormalized stress tensor away from the impulsive surface. Smooth-front regularizations of both profiles reproduce, at the calculated orders and away from the characteristic zero mode, the singular structure of the matched impulsive propagators without generating inverse transverse-momentum poles.
