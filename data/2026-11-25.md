### [1] [Spectroscopic identification of z>7 quasars with Roman Space Telescope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31352)
*Mainak Singha,Wei Leong Tee,Sangeeta Malhotra,Isak Wold,James Rhoads,Xiaohui Fan*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 本文模拟Roman空间望远镜宽场仪器(RWFI)无缝光谱在高红移类星体搜寻中的表现：z=7.5–10类星体的Lyman-α断裂完备度在J_AB≈23.7–24.1仍高于90%，50%完备度位于J_AB≈24.3–24.7；J_AB≳25时结果强烈依赖所采用的Lyman-α等值宽分布，尤其依赖其高EW尾部。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RWFI无缝光谱覆盖1.0–1.93 μm的大视场，使直接搜寻z>7类星体的Lyman-α断裂成为可能，但其在高红移、暗端条件下的探测完备度与分类可靠性尚需定量评估。

Method: 采用类HLWAS曝光条件、类ESpRESSO模拟流程生成z=7.5–10类星体光谱：包含PSF卷积、无缝色散、源与背景的泊松噪声、读出噪声，并进行Horne最优提取；使用三种Lyman-α等值宽(EW)预设以包络线强度不确定性；再用光谱角映射(SAM)将提取谱与M/L/T矮星及被动红星系模板比较。

Result: Lyman-α断裂完备度在J_AB≈23.7–24.1仍>90%，在J_AB≈24.3–24.7降至50%；J_AB≳25时结果强烈依赖EW分布。固定红移SAM测试中类星体检出率为TPR≥0.982，加噪的褐矮星与红星系测试谱均未被误判为类星体。但由于测试谱与参考类来自同一有限母模板库，该结果反映的是库内噪声稳定性，而非独立定标的假阳性率。

Conclusion: RWFI无缝光谱可在J_AB≲24量级高效、纯净地识别z≈7.5–10类星体；暗端(J_AB≳25)探测能力受Lyman-α EW分布高尾主导，是未来模型不确定性的关键来源，且需独立样本给出真实假阳性率。

Abstract: The Roman Wide Field Instrument grism will cover a large area over 1.0-1.93 micrometers, making it possible to search directly for Lyman-alpha breaks in quasars beyond redshift z = 7. We simulate quasars at z = 7.5-10 under HLWAS-like exposures with ESpRESSO, including PSF convolution, slitless dispersion, source and background Poisson noise, read noise, and Horne optimal extraction. Three Lyman-alpha equivalent-width (EW) prescriptions are used to bracket the uncertainty in line strength, and the extracted spectra are compared with M/L/T dwarfs and passive red galaxies using the spectral angle mapper. The Lyman-alpha-break completeness remains above 90% to J_AB approximately 23.7-24.1 and reaches 50% at J_AB approximately 24.3-24.7. Beyond J_AB approximately 25, the result depends strongly on the adopted EW distribution. In the fixed-redshift SAM test, the QSO recovery rate is TPR >= 0.982. None of the noisy brown-dwarf or red-galaxy test spectra is classified as a QSO. Because those test spectra are generated from the same finite parent libraries as the reference classes, this result measures the stability of the classification against noise within those libraries rather than an independently calibrated survey false-positive rate. At the faint end, the strongest astrophysical sensitivity in the present calculation is to the high-EW tail of the Lyman-alpha distribution.

### [2] [JWST/NIRSpec observations of AMORE6 suggest active Pop~III star formation in a self-polluted galaxy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31355)
*Mirko Curti,Elka Rusta,Stefania Salvadori,Stefano Carniani,Francesco D'Eugenio,Bianca Moreschini,Roberto Maiolino,Alessandro Marconi,Giacomo Venturi,Francesco Belfiore,Elisa Cataldi,Viola Gelli,Yuki Isobe,Xihan Ji,Mingyu Li,Robert G. Pascalau,James A. A. Trussler,Hannah Übler*

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AMORE6此前因NIRCam无狭缝光谱缺失[OIII]λ5007而被提名为"无金属"候选，但该判据并不充分：缺乏金属线也可能源于电离源性质差异而非零金属丰度，且此前缺少对关键复合线（HeII、CIV等）的深度探测与对电离源的定量鉴别。本文旨在通过深NIRSpec光谱直接检验其化学丰度、电离源性质，并利用HeII线约束Pop III星族的IMF形状。

Method: 对更亮像AMORE6-B（μ≈78，z=5.725）进行JWST/NIRSpec深光谱观测，探测HeII λλ1640,4686、CIV λλ1548,51、Hβ及弱[OIII]（R3=0.25）；由H/He线得到T_e≈2×10^4 K、Y=0.226、可忽略的衰减；用多种定标给出氧丰度并比较C/O。进而构建不同电离源（金属贫乏星族、极 massive stars、吸积型源、贫金属恒星+硬X射线源、Pop III等）的光致电离模型，并进行自污染情景下的全SED拟合，同时用HeII/Hβ比值联合约束Pop III IMF的斜率x与M_ch。

Conclusion: AMORE6-B并非严格无金属，而是极端低氧丰度、C/O近太阳的星系；其光谱可由处于自污染阶段、具有top-heavy IMF的Pop III星族主导电离来解释，且HeII/Balmer比首次将Pop III IMF的特征质量约束到M_ch≲70 M⊙（Salpeter斜率），排除了最极端top-heavy情形。X射线双星模型在物理上自洽性较差，可作为次要解释。

Abstract: AMORE6 is a strongly lensed ($μ\simeq78$), intrinsically faint galaxy at $z=5.725$, previously proposed as a metal-free candidate from the absence of [OIII]$λ$5007 in NIRCam slitless spectroscopy. We present deep JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy of its brighter image, AMORE6-B. We detect HeII$λλ$1640,4686, with HeII$λ$4686/H$β=0.080\pm0.015$ and EW(HeII$λ$4686)$=35\pm8$ Å, weak [OIII] ($R_3=0.25$) and CIV$λλ$1548,51, and no other metal lines. H and He lines give negligible attenuation, $T_e\simeq2\times10^4$ K, and $Y=0.226^{+0.022}_{-0.024}$. The oxygen abundance, $12+\log(O/H)\simeq6.08^{+0.24}_{-0.80}$ (0.04-0.4% $Z_\odot$, depending on the calibration), is the lowest at its stellar mass, while C/O is near or above solar. Photoionization models of different ionizing sources favour metal-free stars with a top-heavy initial mass function (IMF), as in the self-polluted phase of simulated Population III (Pop III) galaxies, or a metal-poor population with a luminous hard X-ray source; ordinary metal-poor populations, very massive stars, and accretion-based sources are disfavoured. A full SED fit in the fiducial self-polluted scenario gives $1.3\times10^5\,M_\odot$ of Pop III stars formed within the last 4 Myr, which ionize mildly enriched gas and leak 86% of their ionizing photons. The HeII-to-Balmer ratio constrains the slope $x$ and characteristic mass $M_{ch}$ of the Pop III IMF jointly, to $90x-268\lesssim M_{ch}/M_\odot\lesssim172x-330$ ($3σ$; $M_{ch}\lesssim70\,M_\odot$ for a Salpeter slope), excluding the most top-heavy IMFs allowed by near-field constraints. X-ray binaries are a possible alternative, but require either a metal-poor population too young (1 Myr) to host compact objects and an X-ray luminosity far above that of metal-poor galaxies, or $2\times10^6\,M_\odot$ of older Pop III.2 stars whose metals were mostly expelled.

Result: 对变形位移链，扩展复杂度等于平均激发数，其速率对应变形动量正交分量，加速度为C̈_K=2ω²⟨[a,a†]⟩，即变形对易子充当Krylov链上的"力"，平均变形量控制早期增长。四种区域：(i) max(p,q)<1时复杂度有界，属代数本身导致的局域化；(ii) p=1,q<1时弹道增长，渐近速度由theta型级数精确给出；(iii) p=q=1时按t²增长；(iv) max(p,q)>1时Jacobi算子非本质自伴、矩问题不定，Lanczos系数不再决定动力学——偶/奇截断收敛到两个不同的反射对称N-极值解，奇截断带有一个闭式权重的零能原子；自伴扩张给出在早期重合、当Krylov波前到达无穷后才分离的一族复杂度曲线。

Conclusion: Krylov动力学即正交多项式与Jacobi算子理论：(p,q)-变形振子族统一了多种Krylov链并给出清晰的四区分类。关键结论是：当max(p,q)>1时，Krylov复杂度只有在无穷Krylov指标处指定边界条件后才被良好定义。附录给出正交多项式流程及在精确可解链上的验证。

Abstract: Krylov and spread complexity map quantum dynamics onto a quantum walk on a half-line whose hopping amplitudes are the Lanczos coefficients, so that Krylov dynamics is the theory of orthogonal polynomials and Jacobi operators. We use this correspondence to study Krylov chains generated by the two-parameter $(p,q)$-deformed oscillator, which interpolates between the Heisenberg--Weyl chain, the $q$-oscillator chain of double-scaled SYK, and chains with exponentially growing Lanczos coefficients. For the deformed displacement chain the spread complexity is the mean excitation number, its rate is the deformed momentum quadrature, and its acceleration is $\ddot C_K=2ω^2\langle[a,a^\dagger]\rangle$, so the deformed commutator acts as a force along the Krylov chain and the mean deformation controls early-time growth. The deformation parameters separate four regimes. For $\max(p,q)<1$ the complexity stays bounded, a localization produced by the algebra alone. For $p=1$, $q<1$ it grows ballistically, with an exact asymptotic velocity given by a theta-type series. At $p=q=1$ it grows as $t^2$. For $\max(p,q)>1$ the Jacobi operator is not essentially self-adjoint, the moment problem is indeterminate, and the Lanczos coefficients no longer determine the dynamics. Even and odd truncations converge to two different evolutions, the two reflection-symmetric N-extremal solutions of the moment problem, the odd one carrying a zero-energy atom of closed-form weight; the self-adjoint extensions yield a family of complexity curves that coincide at early times and separate once the Krylov wavefront reaches infinity. Krylov complexity in this regime is thus defined only once a boundary condition at infinite Krylov index is specified. The orthogonal-polynomial procedure and its checks on exactly solvable chains are given in the appendices.
